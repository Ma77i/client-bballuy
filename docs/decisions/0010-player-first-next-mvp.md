# ADR 0010 - Player-first MVP before iOS beta

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: accepted for audience and sequencing.

## Context

SPEC-001 supplies the verified local account/court/community journey. The owner
now wants to help players looking to join others first, and defer iOS beta work
until after the next MVP. Earlier beta-first instructions are superseded.

## Decision

Prioritize SPEC-002, a player-first pickup/session and match MVP. Its primary journey
is find an upcoming opportunity to play, inspect it, join or leave, and return
to personal upcoming sessions without needing a permanent team.

SPEC-002 identifies only the player-first pickup MVP. At the owner's request,
remove the beta specification, tasks, preparation evidence and registry entry.
Do not reserve a specification ID or retain a beta prerequisite task. Define
beta work later, after this MVP, against the product then accepted.

The audience and order are confirmed. SPEC-002/T00 subsequently resolved the
first delivery's rules; it is ready for local contract implementation in T01.
This decision does not approve every suggestion in the idea backlog.

## Alternatives and consequences

Beta-first would distribute a narrower experience; it is explicitly deferred.
Team-first would serve existing squads; it is not the selected initial audience.
Teams, feeds, rankings and tournaments remain later ideas. Sessions require
organizers to supply activity even though joining players are the main audience.
Local iPhone/Expo Go acceptance still matters during MVP work; deferring beta
does not mean skipping device or server-permission verification.

No application behavior, schema, hosted deployment or publication changes follow
automatically from this planning decision.
